About

The Inkwell

The Inkwell is an independent publication for people who still believe that words are worth slowing down for. We publish long-form essays, considered criticism, and dispatches from wherever the writing is good.

We don't chase trends. We don't optimize for engagement. We write about things because they interest us deeply, and we trust that deep interest is contagious.

Our contributors come from journalism, academia, design, technology, and the productive margins between them. What they share is a commitment to writing that respects the reader's intelligence and time — which means not wasting either.

What We Publish

Long reads on technology and its cultural consequences. Essays on design and the built world. Travel writing that actually goes somewhere. Cultural criticism that has a point of view.

We publish when we have something worth saying. Not on a schedule. Not to fill a quota. When the work is ready.

The Name

An inkwell is the vessel that holds the material of writing before it becomes something. We like the implication: this is a place where ideas are held, prepared, and then released into the world in a form someone might actually want to read.

Write for Us

We're always interested in hearing from writers with something urgent to say. If you have a piece or a pitch, we'd love to hear it. We read everything we receive and respond to what interests us.
